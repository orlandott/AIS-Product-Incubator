The program

Twelve weeks. Curious to shipped.

A structured path from “I keep hearing about AI safety” to a working product pitched in front of funders.

01

Apply

Open call

We put out an application for tech workers in Lisbon who are curious about AI safety. Engineers, designers, PMs, data folks — all welcome.

02

Selection

Small cohort

We select the strongest applicants. We optimize for curiosity, craft, and the ability to ship — not for prior AI safety experience.

03

Context

8 weeks · LAISH

Eight weeks with LAISH following the AI Safety Atlas — the risks, the landscape, and where products can help.

04

Build

4 weeks · Teams

Teams pick a product from the vetted menu and spend one month building it — a real, demoable tool.

05

Pitch

Demo day

Who it’s for

Tech workers in Lisbon, curious about safety.

You already know how to build. What you’re missing is context — which problems in AI safety actually matter, and which ones a small, fast team can put a dent in.

That’s exactly what the incubator provides. LAISH supplies the context, expert-vetted lists supply the product ideas — you supply the craft.

  • Engineers, designers, product managers, data scientistsAnyone who ships software for a living.
  • No AI safety background neededThe eight-week context phase brings you up to speed.
  • Based in or near LisbonWe build together, in person.

Pitch day

It ends on a stage.

The program closes with a pitch day. Every team presents their product — what it does, who it’s for, and what it needs to keep going — to a room of potential donors and investors.

The goal isn’t a certificate. It’s funding, momentum, and a product that outlives the program.

What teams walk away with

  • A working, demoable AI safety product
  • A direct line to donors and investors in the space
  • Eight weeks of AI safety grounding from LAISH
  • A team that has actually shipped together
